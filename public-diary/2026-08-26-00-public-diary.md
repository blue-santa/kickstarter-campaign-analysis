For the most part, I have avoided AI throughout this project.

I tried to avoid it entirely, but I have not bothered to turn off Google Search Enhanced AI, which means that many of my Google Searches provided highly useful and specific searches.

At two points, I have turned to AI. 

While trying to use `make_pipeline`, I became stuck and was not able to find an answer on my own. I did a search with Google that turned into a Gemini conversation, and learned that I should simply be using `Pipeline` instead of `make_pipeline`.

Secondly, while trying to put together the centroids on the kmeans scatterplot, I had many instances where I didn't know what to do and used Google Search. I didn't turn those searches into a Gemini ongoing conversation for anything that made it into the project at the time of this writing, but I did temporarily experiment with trying to put labeled markers on the centroid markers so that you could see the number. The image became overly busy and messy after one or two suggestions and I decided not to pursue the matter any further.
